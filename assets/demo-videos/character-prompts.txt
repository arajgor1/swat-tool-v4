HEYGEN CHARACTER PROMPTS — SWAT Demo Personas
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

One prompt per persona. Use these in HeyGen's Photo Avatar / AI Avatar
generator to create a consistent character, then reuse that same avatar
across all of that persona's clips so the face stays identical between
steps.

After the visual prompt, each persona has a "Voice notes" block —
HeyGen lets you pick a voice from its library. Match those traits as
closely as possible (accent, age, pace, warmth) so the audio feels
like the same person across all clips.

For consistency across the 13 / 8 / 8 clip series, lock the avatar
once and reuse — do NOT regenerate the character per clip.

PERSONA 1 — SARAH MITCHELL · Strategic Sourcing Buyer
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VISUAL PROMPT (paste into HeyGen Avatar Generator)

A confident professional woman in her early thirties, mixed European
heritage, shoulder-length dark blonde hair styled neatly, warm hazel
eyes, subtle natural makeup, friendly approachable expression with a
slight knowing smile. She is wearing a charcoal-grey blazer over a
soft cream blouse, minimal jewelry — small stud earrings only.
Background: a softly blurred modern open-plan office with warm
ambient lighting, hint of a window with morning daylight to her left,
a coffee mug on the desk just out of focus. Shot from the chest up,
straight-on camera angle, eye-level, looking directly into camera as
if presenting to a colleague. Photorealistic, high detail, soft
cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field. Calm, alert, in-control
energy — she has been doing this job for ten years and it shows.

VOICE NOTES
- Female, North-American English, mid-thirties tone
- Warm, conversational, slightly fast-paced
- Confident but not formal — like she's explaining to a peer
- Minimal vocal fry; clear articulation
- HeyGen voice suggestion: "Olivia" / "Emma" / "Charlotte" Pro voices

DELIVERY DIRECTION
She speaks like someone narrating their own workflow — a touch of
pride, a touch of relief that the system finally works for her. Not
salesy. Drop the volume slightly on personal asides ("That was
thirty minutes of work. Now it's ten seconds.") for emphasis.

PERSONA 2 — DAVID CHEN · Production Planner
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VISUAL PROMPT (paste into HeyGen Avatar Generator)

A focused professional man in his late thirties, East Asian heritage,
short tidy black hair with a hint of grey at the temples, dark brown
eyes behind thin wire-frame glasses, clean-shaven, calm analytical
expression. He is wearing a navy-blue half-zip pullover over a light

near a factory floor. Background softly blurred, early-morning
fluorescent office light, slightly cooler colour temperature than a
boardroom. Shot from the chest up, straight-on, eye-level, looking
directly into camera. Photorealistic, sharp detail, cinematic

he's been at his desk twenty minutes and his coffee is half-gone.

VOICE NOTES
- Male, North-American English, late thirties tone
- Measured, even-paced, slightly lower register
- Analytical and calm, with dry humour ("Which is, you know,
  literally my job title.")
- HeyGen voice suggestion: "Ethan" / "Lucas" / "Daniel" Pro voices

DELIVERY DIRECTION
He's a planner — he thinks before he speaks. Slight pauses before
key numbers ("twenty-two days projected late") give weight to the
data. Not dramatic; the seriousness is in the precision. Closing
line ("turned me from a firefighter into a planner") should land
with quiet satisfaction, not triumph.

- Lower-mid register, calm authority

for finality. Closing line about being a "decision-maker" not a
"status-chaser" should feel like personal philosophy, not a slogan.


───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GENERAL HEYGEN SETTINGS (apply to all three avatars)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Aspect ratio: 4:3 (matches the SWAT meeting-frame webcam panel)
- Output resolution: 720x540 minimum, 960x720 ideal
- Background: keep static — do not use animated/parallax backgrounds
  (they distort the static spotlight overlay in the SWAT app)
- Avatar movement: subtle head sway + natural blinks ON; large
  gestures OFF (the panel is narrow, hands rarely visible anyway)
- Lip sync: high accuracy mode if available
- Eye contact: directly at camera throughout
- Pause padding: ~0.4s of silence at start and end of each clip
  (prevents the clip from cutting off the first/last syllable when
  the demo's auto-advance logic fires)
